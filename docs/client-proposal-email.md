## Tips for Personalization

- **Cold outreach:** Always include a real generated response to one of their actual unanswered reviews — this is the hook that gets replies
- **Subject line:** Mention their specific business name, not a generic subject
- **Keep it short:** Romanian business owners respond better to concrete examples than feature lists
- **Timing:** Send cold outreach Tuesday–Thursday, 09:00–11:00 — highest open rates
- **Follow-up:** If no reply in 5 days, one follow-up: *"Ați avut ocazia să citiți mesajul meu? Aș vrea să știu dacă răspunsul generat vi s-a părut util."*
